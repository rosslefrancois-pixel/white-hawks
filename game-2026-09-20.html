All of it in the first

Game 2 · Called as it happened

Good afternoon from the Hemenway Rink at Choate, where the White Hawks are on the road against the Connecticut Polar Bears — and where, if you came in late, you missed the entire scoring output of the Worcester White Hawks.

Because it all happens in the opening period. 7:57 remaining — CLAIRE LEFRANCOIS, on her own, nobody to hand an assist to. One-nothing Worcester.

3:07 to go — SLOAN GENDRON, also unassisted, and it's two. And with 2:03 left in that very same period, Gendron AGAIN! Her second of the afternoon, the third Worcester goal of the period, and not one of the three needed a helper. Three goals, three solo efforts. You do not see that on a scoresheet very often.

Second period, and the Polar Bears begin to lean on them. Camryn Connor to the box for hooking at 11:03 — the only penalty Worcester takes all afternoon — and the Hawks kill it off. But with twenty-nine seconds left in that second period, LEAH CHEN, from Helena Dulaney, and Connecticut has a pulse heading to the third.

And that is where Elizabeth Brigham settles this. The full forty-five minutes for the Worcester netminder, a third period in which the Polar Bears get nothing at all, and a hockey game that ends the way the first period said it would.

One penalty for Worcester, none at all for the Polar Bears — a clean, quick afternoon, seventy minutes from puck drop to handshake. Gendron's first of the two, at 3:07 of the first, stands as your game-winner. And Sloan Gendron now has four goals in two games. The White Hawks are two-and-oh.
